Rules

Full PPR scoring with 6-point passing touchdowns. Players earn 1 point per 10 rushing or receiving yards, 1 point per reception, and 1 point per 25 passing yards. Defensive scoring rewards sacks and takeaways, with additional bonuses and penalties based on points and total yards allowed.

One keeper per club. Each of the 12 teams locks a single player into the Szn 10 draft. The round listed with that player is the pick you surrender; the rest of the snake stays live. Keepers locked August 30. The Szn 10 draft completed August 31.

If you kept someone last year who was your 1st-round pick, you can no longer keep them. If you are keeping someone for the 2nd year in a row, their draft round moves up this year. Example: keeping Saquon as a 2nd-rounder last year and keeping him again this year costs a 1st-round pick. A used round is skipped on the board so that club does not pick twice in the same round.

The locked list lives on the League page. Rules here stay the house wording only.
