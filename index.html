Rules

Full PPR scoring with 6-point passing touchdowns. Players earn 1 point per 10 rushing or receiving yards, 1 point per reception, and 1 point per 25 passing yards. Defensive scoring rewards sacks and takeaways, with additional bonuses and penalties based on points and total yards allowed.
